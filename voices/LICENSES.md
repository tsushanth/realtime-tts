# Piper checkpoint licence audit

Source of all checkpoints: HuggingFace dataset `rhasspy/piper-checkpoints` (MODEL_CARD per voice, read 2026-09-20).
Where a card said "See URL" / "see LICENSE" the linked page was fetched; results below. This is an engineering
audit, not legal advice.

## Verdict key

- **OK** - public domain / CC0 / permissive, and the model is trained from scratch or from a clean base.
- **OK+attr** - CC BY (attribution text must be shipped/shown).
- **FLAGGED (Lessac base)** - dataset licence is fine, but the checkpoint was *finetuned from Piper's Lessac
  checkpoint*. The Lessac data licence (https://www.cstr.ed.ac.uk/projects/blizzard/2013/lessac_blizzard2013/license.html)
  grants use "exclusively for Research Purposes only", defined as excluding "any commercial purpose" and covering things
  "using, incorporating or based upon the Materials (in whole or in part)", with no right to sub-licence (cl. 3.2).
  Whether finetuned weights count as "based upon" is untested; the rhasspy model cards do not address it. Treat as a
  real risk for a commercial service. These voices are exported (technically working) but should not be published
  until someone makes a call. Note: the task brief listed joe/mike as OK based on their dataset licence - that holds for
  the data, not for the Lessac lineage.
- **NOT OK** - NC, SA with unclear obligations, restrictive, unknown/unreachable licence, or NC-lineage.

## Bottom line

Voices with clean lineage and clean data (exported): en-us-ljspeech, en-us-kristin, en-us-john (no attribution needed),
de-de-mls, fr-fr-mls, nl-nl-mls (CC BY attribution), it-it-serena (CC BY, synthetic-data caveat).
Everything else exported is FLAGGED (Lessac base) pending a decision. es, pt, ru, pl, hi, tr, zh, ja, ar have
**no** clean-lineage voice in this repo. Exported voices' owner.json `license` field states the dataset licence only;
the Lessac-lineage flag is recorded here, not in owner.json.
